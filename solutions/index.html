01

AI Organisation Solutions

CorpOS in active development

Management tools built on how work changes in the age of AI — enabling companies founded today, and helping established businesses make the transition.

AI is not only making old processes faster; it is changing the shape of the organisation itself. Once an AI teammate can hold a real role with real responsibilities, the question of how a company should be structured has a new answer.

We work with two kinds of company: those founded in the age of AI, which can be AI-native from day one, and established businesses that need a route into AI which does not require tearing out what already works.

Solutions
CorpOS (built in-house) — an AI-native management platform entered through instant messaging. The organisation is made up of human colleagues and AI teammates: each AI teammate holds a defined role, defined responsibilities and a defined reporting line, working alongside people in the same space
Your existing messaging platform + a CorpOS team of AI teammates — keep the platform your company already runs on, such as Lark, and add AI teammates into it to take on the work that can be automated, one role at a time
Where it applies
Companies founded in the age of AI — build the team as human colleagues and AI teammates from day one
Growing small and mid-sized businesses — add capacity through AI teammates instead of headcount alone
Established businesses adopting AI — bring AI into daily operations without replacing existing systems

CorpOS is under active development, and we are refining it with a small number of design partners. If you would like to be an early user, we would like to hear from you.

Industry Management Solutions

Either AI teammates who hold defined roles, or AI assistance layered onto existing work — a new operating model for a specific industry, and AI capability for the systems it already runs.

Generic software struggles to match the operating reality of a specific industry, and industry software often stops at recording what already happened. We work deep inside particular industries and build AI capability directly into the business system.

There are two ways in: AI teammates who take on roles within the process, or AI assistance that strengthens the systems and the people already in place.

VIN-level inventory with in-transit, in-bond and in-showroom states
Purchase, shipping, customs and landed-cost tracking per unit
Multi-currency purchasing and settlement, with VAT treatment appropriate to UAE requirements
Chassis-to-invoice traceability for audit and warranty claims
Sales pipeline, reservations, deposits and delivery handover
Flying — cross-border trading system for construction fit-out (tailored delivery)
End-to-end cross-border procurement and trading for the fit-out industry
Supplier, quotation and order collaboration across currencies and languages
Logistics and customs milestone tracking, with delivery dates visible throughout
Cost captured and settled at project level
Delivered as a tailored solution, matching how a particular business trades and runs its projects
Across both
AI teammates or AI assistance, whichever suits the team as it is today
Arabic, English and Chinese interfaces with correct right-to-left layout
Deployment on your infrastructure or a hosting arrangement we manage
Documented APIs so the system is never a dead end

CarSaas is in development and expected to launch shortly. Flying is delivered as a tailored solution and can be adapted to how a particular business trades and runs its projects — tell us about your operation and we will say what would carry over.
